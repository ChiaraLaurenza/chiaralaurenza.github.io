About

Contemporary and jazz dancer, painter and choreographer, based in Munich.

Portrait coming soon

I am Chiara, a dancer and painter. I trained in contemporary dance, jazz and ballet at Iwanson in Munich, and I paint in acrylics.

In my Art & Dance Space, movement comes first: we dance a feeling, pause, and let the body decide the colours on the canvas.

Before dance became my path, I studied bioinformatics at TUM and LMU. It taught me to look closely at what happens inside a system — today, that system is the body.
