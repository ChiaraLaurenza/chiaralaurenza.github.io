Dance &
Art

I take a concept into the body, dance it, pause, and translate what the body felt into colour. The painting is not a trace of the movement — it is a translation of the feeling.

The Process

ChooseStep 1
A concept to explore — a memory, a border, letting go.
EmbodyStep 2
Dance the concept until the body knows it.
PauseStep 3
Stay still and listen: weight, warmth, tension, space. What is left in the body?
TranslateStep 4
Colour, pressure, rhythm and density on canvas — chosen only after the dance, never before.
ReflectStep 5
What came through? What was lost on the way?
